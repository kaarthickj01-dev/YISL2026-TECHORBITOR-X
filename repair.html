MECHVR

Hands‑On Automobile & Vehicle Repair Simulator — Cars · Bikes · Planes · Jets · Spaceships

Works with mouse/touch on desktop & mobile, and with VR headsets via WebXR (use the 🥽 button, top‑right).

How To Play

1. Pick a vehicle type, then a brand & its latest model, then a paint color.
2. You'll be placed in a repair bay next to the vehicle with its bay panel open.
3. A translucent Ghost Hand demonstrates 5 repair movements, one at a time.
4. Watch carefully, then repeat by clicking/tapping (or VR‑pointing at) the glowing bolts — do all 5.
5. Higher levels add more rounds of 5 moves, and require the correct order.
6. Some moves need a part bought from the Shop using CarBucks (₡).
7. Hit Start Engine — get it right and the engine roars to life (+₡); get it wrong and it blows up (−₡).
8. Fix 2 vehicles of a brand successfully to level up — each vehicle brand levels independently.
